# Analysis of the relational-model expansion

Folded into the local handoff. Verdict: keep it, but only the operational cuts.

The inventory step is the right correction. "Find someone I know" is still hunting. "List the 150 before you buy a stranger" is a starting asset. Vote sort stays. Act and carry are added. A soft voter can be a strong carrier.

Mackay earns his place as continuity, not as a 66-question form. Connection means the next conversation starts where the last one ended. The dossier warning is the constraint. Store what the relationship allows and what the next conversation needs.

Granovetter is the real upgrade. Cold is not the absence of a door. Cold is the absence of a path. Order: strong tie, useful weak tie, then cold. Record the edge: type, strength, bridge, asked, completed, connection resulted. Do not assume a weak tie transfers trust. Measure it.

Ganz belongs between Cooperation and Conversion, as self / us / now, not as a speech. The ask stays voluntary and right-sized.

Cialdini is diagnostic. Reciprocity, liking, specific social proof, small voluntary commitment, unity. If it only works as a trick, leave it out.

Gitomer, Voss, Alinsky, and Kalla/Broockman were already in the model. Do not restack them as eight equal authorities.

Software requirement: node, edge, and network are three records. COLD to REFERRED is a state change the system enforces. New metrics are hypotheses: strong-tie completion, weak-tie completion, trust-transfer by edge type, bridge yield, cold displacement, continuity, participation progression.

One line: Inventory the people you already know. Remember what matters to them. Map the relationships that connect them to others. Connect. Cooperate. Convert. Let them carry the work across strong ties and useful weak ones, using whatever medium fits. Go cold only where no relational path reaches. Establish a beachhead, make it warm, and begin again.
