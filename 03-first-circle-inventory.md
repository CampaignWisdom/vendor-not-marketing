# First-circle inventory

Insert this before "The model begins with the person you know best."

Do not start by hunting for one person you happen to know. Start by inventorying the relational capital you already possess, before you buy any new contact.

The Reputation slide already named the job: literally list your ~150 relationships. Friends, family, neighbors, co-workers, church family, business associations, group members. Every candidate, volunteer, activist, and organizer builds that first-circle map.

Those names are not a voter file. They are entry points. Sort them on two questions, not one. Who might participate. Who might carry you into a network the organization does not currently reach. A person can be a weak voter and a strong carrier. Do not throw that name out because the vote score is soft.

Only after that map exists do you pick the first conversation. The model begins with the person you know best on that list, not with a stranger the list vendor sold you.

The first step is not "find someone I know." The first step is "inventory the relational capital we already possess before buying any new contact."
